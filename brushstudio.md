# Privacy Policy — DotShake Brush Studio

_Last updated: 2026-08-02_

Brush Studio does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. We
operate no servers and no accounts — there is nowhere for your data to go
except the places described below, all of which you control.

## What stays on your device

The brushes you make and edit, and your settings, are stored in the app's own
storage on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

## iCloud sync

If you enable syncing, your custom brushes are stored in **your own private
iCloud database** using Apple's CloudKit.

- The data lives in your personal iCloud account, under your Apple Account,
  and counts against your iCloud storage.
- **We cannot see it.** A private CloudKit database is accessible only to
  the account that owns it; the developer has no access to its contents.
- Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy govern that storage.
- Turning sync off stops further uploads; removing the data is done through
  iOS Settings › your name › iCloud.

## No account, no tracking, no analytics

Brush Studio has no sign-in of its own and no user profiles, and includes no
analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not know who you are or what
you make.

## Import and export

Exporting a brush writes a file where you choose to put it; importing reads
one you picked. Both happen only when you ask for them, and the file goes
only where you send it.

## Purchases

Brush Studio has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

## Children

Brush Studio is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address
with a new date.

chenzhendeveloper@gmail.com

# 隐私政策 — DotShake Brush Studio

_最后更新:2026-08-02_

Brush Studio 不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息。我们不运营任何服务器,
也没有账号体系 —— 除了下文列出、且完全由你掌控的去处之外,你的数据无处
可去。

## 留在设备上的部分

你制作和修改的笔刷,以及偏好设置,保存在 App 自身的存储中。删除 App
即随之删除。

## iCloud 同步

若你开启同步,你的自制笔刷会通过 Apple 的 CloudKit 存入**你自己的 iCloud
私有数据库**。

- 数据位于你个人的 iCloud 账户下,占用你的 iCloud 存储空间。
- **我们无法查看这些内容。** CloudKit 私有数据库仅对其所属账户可见,
  开发者没有访问权限。
- 该存储适用 Apple 的 iCloud 条款与隐私政策。
- 关闭同步即停止后续上传;删除已同步的数据请在 iOS「设置 › 你的姓名 ›
  iCloud」中操作。

## 没有账号、没有追踪、没有分析

本 App 自身没有登录功能,不包含任何分析、广告或追踪 SDK。我们不知道你是谁,
也不知道你做了什么笔刷。

## 导入与导出

导出笔刷时,文件写到你指定的位置;导入时,读取的是你自己选择的文件。
两者都只在你主动操作时发生,文件也只会去往你指定的地方。

## 关于购买

Brush Studio 没有 App 内购买,也没有订阅。

## 儿童隐私

本 App 并非面向儿童设计,且不收集任何人的数据。

## 政策变更

如本政策发生变更,更新后的版本将发布于本页面并标注新日期。

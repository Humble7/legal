# Privacy Policy — Dottelier

_Last updated: 2026-08-01_

Dottelier does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.
The app has no servers, no accounts, and no network features at all.

## Your photos stay on your device

Photos you select are read, edited, and rendered entirely on your device.
They are never uploaded to us or to any third party. Exported images and
videos go only where you send them: your photo library, or an app you
choose in the share sheet.

## No account, no tracking, no analytics

Dottelier has no sign-in and no user profiles, and includes no analytics,
advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not know who you are or what you make.

## Photo library access

Dottelier asks for photo access so you can pick photos, and for permission
to add images to your library when you export. Both can be changed any time
in iOS Settings.

## Sending an artwork to Glello

If you send an artwork to Glello (our companion app), the transfer happens
entirely on your device between the two apps. Nothing leaves the device.

## Data on your device

Your artworks, settings, and purchase state are stored in the app's own
storage. Deleting the app removes them. If you use Apple's device backup,
that copy is governed by Apple's terms, not ours.

## Purchases

Dottelier Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the
payment; we never receive your payment details. The app stores only whether
your purchase is currently active, on your device.

## Children

Dottelier is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address
with a new date.

# 隐私政策 — 朵朵影记

_最后更新:2026-08-01_

朵朵影记不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息。本 App 没有服务器、没有账号,
也不含任何联网功能。

## 照片始终留在你的设备上

你选择的照片全部在本机读取、编辑与渲染,不会上传给我们或任何第三方。
导出的图片与视频只会去往你指定的位置:你的相册,或你在分享面板中选择的
App。

## 没有账号、没有追踪、没有分析

本 App 没有登录功能,不包含任何分析、广告或追踪 SDK。我们不知道你是谁,
也不知道你创作了什么。

## 相册权限

App 需要相册权限用于选取照片,以及在导出时向相册添加内容。两项权限均可
随时在 iOS 设置中更改。

## 将作品发送到 Glello

若你把作品发送到我们的姊妹 App Glello,传输完全在你的设备上、两个 App
之间完成,数据不会离开设备。

## 设备上的数据

你的作品、偏好设置与购买状态保存在 App 自身的存储中,删除 App 即随之删除。
若你使用 Apple 的设备备份,该备份副本适用 Apple 的条款。

## 关于购买

朵朵影记 Pro 通过 Apple 的 App 内购买销售,款项由 Apple 处理,我们不会
接触你的支付信息。App 仅在本机保存「当前是否已购买」这一状态。

## 儿童隐私

本 App 并非面向儿童设计,且不收集任何人的数据。

## 政策变更

如本政策发生变更,更新后的版本将发布于本页面并标注新日期。

chenzhennba@gmail.com

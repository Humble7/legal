# Privacy Policy — DotShake

_Last updated: 2026-08-01_

DotShake does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. We
operate no servers and no accounts — there is nowhere for your data to go
except the places described below, all of which you control.

## What stays on your device

Your drawings, brushes, palettes, and settings are stored in the app's own
storage on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

## iCloud sync

If you enable syncing, your artworks, palettes, and custom brushes are
stored in **your own private iCloud database** using Apple's CloudKit.

- The data lives in your personal iCloud account, under your Apple Account,
  and counts against your iCloud storage.
- **We cannot see it.** A private CloudKit database is accessible only to
  the account that owns it; the developer has no access to its contents.
- Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy govern that storage.
- Turning sync off stops further uploads; removing the data is done through
  iOS Settings › your name › iCloud.

The app also uses Apple's silent push notifications so a change on one of
your devices reaches the others. These carry no message content and are
never used for marketing.

## Drawing with nearby devices

DotShake can share a drawing session with nearby devices, and let them watch.

- The connection is **directly between devices** over your local network or
  Bluetooth. Nothing is routed through us, and no copy is kept anywhere but
  the participating devices.
- It only happens while you actively start or join a session.
- While a session is running, the people you connected with can see the
  canvas you are drawing. Only connect with people you trust.
- iOS asks for local network and Bluetooth permission the first time; both
  can be revoked any time in iOS Settings.

## Exporting

Images, videos, and `.dotshake` files you export go only where you send
them: your photo library, or an app you pick in the share sheet. iOS asks
for permission before anything is added to your photo library.

## Purchases

DotShake Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the
payment; we never receive your payment details. The app stores only whether
your purchase is currently active, on your device.

## No tracking

DotShake contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.
We do not know who you are, what you draw, or how you use the app.

## Children

DotShake is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address
with a new date.

# 隐私政策 — DotShake

_最后更新:2026-08-01_

DotShake 不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息。我们没有服务器,也没有账号
系统——你的数据只会存在于下述这些由你自己控制的位置。

## 留在设备上的数据

你的画作、笔刷、色板与偏好设置保存在 App 自身的存储中,删除 App 即随之
删除。

## iCloud 同步

若你开启同步,你的作品、色板与自定义笔刷会通过 Apple 的 CloudKit 存入
**你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库**。

- 数据位于你个人的 iCloud 账户下,占用你的 iCloud 存储空间。
- **我们无法查看这些内容。** CloudKit 私有数据库仅对其所属账户可见,
  开发者没有访问权限。
- 该存储适用 Apple 的 iCloud 条款与隐私政策。
- 关闭同步即停止后续上传;删除已同步的数据请在 iOS「设置 › 你的姓名 ›
  iCloud」中操作。

App 还会使用 Apple 的静默推送,以便一台设备上的改动能及时送达你的其他
设备。这类推送不含任何消息内容,也绝不用于营销。

## 与附近设备一起绘画

DotShake 可以与附近设备共享绘画过程,或让对方观摩。

- 连接是**设备之间直连**,通过你所在的局域网或蓝牙完成。数据不经过我们,
  除参与设备外不留任何副本。
- 仅在你主动发起或加入会话时才会连接。
- 会话进行期间,与你连接的人可以看到你正在绘制的画布。请只与你信任的人
  连接。
- iOS 会在首次使用时请求本地网络与蓝牙权限,两者均可随时在 iOS 设置中
  关闭。

## 导出

你导出的图片、视频与 `.dotshake` 文件只会去往你指定的位置:你的相册,
或你在分享面板中选择的 App。向相册添加内容前 iOS 会先请求授权。

## 关于购买

DotShake Pro 通过 Apple 的 App 内购买销售,款项由 Apple 处理,我们不会
接触你的支付信息。App 仅在本机保存「当前是否已购买」这一状态。

## 没有追踪

DotShake 不包含任何分析、广告或追踪 SDK。我们不知道你是谁,不知道你画了
什么,也不知道你如何使用这个 App。

## 儿童隐私

DotShake 并非面向儿童设计,且不收集任何人的数据。

## 政策变更

如本政策发生变更,更新后的版本将发布于本页面并标注新日期。
